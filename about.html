About

I'm a data engineer with a degree in Computer Science. I spend my days building pipelines, wrangling schemas, and thinking hard about what it actually means for data infrastructure to be reliable.

This blog is where I write about the practical side of the craft — the tradeoffs nobody documents, the failures worth sharing, and occasionally the elegant solutions. No fluff, no "10 tips" listicles. Just things I'd want to read myself.

When I'm not thinking about data, I'm probably thinking about design, music, or why Y2K aesthetics were actually ahead of their time.
