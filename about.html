About

I'm a data engineer with a BS in Computer Science from UC San Diego. In my experience, learning about data and working with data require vastly different skillsets and considerations, and this blog is where I document my learnings from working with data in the real world.

I write about the hands-on lessons I have learned from working as a Data Engineer on large-scale infrastructural services. Not just the theoretical side of the field, but the real-world implementation tradeoffs, pitfalls, and workarounds. Just practical advice I'd want to read myself.

When I'm not thinking about data, I'm probably playing music, practicing cooking different cuisines, or running 5Ks.
